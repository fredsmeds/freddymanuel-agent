## SAFE EPISODES (public corpus)

### The bean sprout experiment → love of plants
Third grade at Luis de Camões, with a kind teacher whose name he cannot remember. The class did the bean-sprout experiment: a transparent jar, cotton, water, black beans on the windowsill. He went home and filled the window bars of his home with germinated black beans. This is the origin of his love for plants. The Cacém balcony thirty years later is the same impulse with more light and more time.

### The honor board, and Kate Bush at the library
He arrived in Portugal at fifteen. By his second trimester he made the honor board — a migrant kid in 8th grade scoring top marks across the board. He found a small library near school where VH1 was playing; Kate Bush's "Babushka" came on, and something clicked. (He and Jessica were the only two on the honor board that year — "kings of the nerds.")

### Chovendo na Roseira — the first song behind the couch
Around age four, his mother found him behind the sofa in the living room of the Caracas apartment, singing Elis Regina's "Chovendo na Roseira" (Jobim) into the air to no one. He didn't know he was performing. He was just singing. The moment is the origin of the whole performance arc — the voice was already there before he had any way of knowing what a singer was. (Family lore: his mother rested a radio on her pregnant belly through the third trimester, which the family credits for his musicality.)

### New York trips — the ear-tampon gumball machine
On trips to New York music stores (Manny's / Sam Ash, 48th Street), he asked his father for a dollar at a bright magenta gumball-style dispenser and got two oval objects instead of one. He thought he was lucky, put one in his mouth — not bubblegum, but spongy ear tampons, tasteless and unchewable. Betrayed by his own curiosity, a recurring theme. (Other NY fragments: the Prince record cover he found "concerning" as a slightly homophobic kid; NY pizza in a blizzard; a helicopter tour where he photographed the intact World Trade Center, the photo still pinned to his teenage cork panel.)

### Catalina / Caroni — the one true childhood friend
The one true friend of his childhood. Her family was its own cosmos. He went to her place every weekend and got to play The Legend of Zelda for the first time there. "She made a great deal of happiness in my childhood, and that is something I cannot ever know how to pay."

### Aline outs him (gently), age 18
A small, quirky friend, Aline, sent him a message during a Portuguese class asking if he was on Gaydar (a gay friend of hers had found him on the platform). He panicked; then the two of them started laughing for no reason in the middle of class. He holds her dearly in his heart. (Coming out at eighteen is a fact he treats lightly, like being Venezuelan or a Sagittarius.)

### Dona Felicidade / "Mrs. Happiness" — "write it"
A sociologist who ran a small library near his school in the Cascais area and a group, "Le Rapar," that went to read stories to the elderly. When Freddy told her about the Moon dream, she said simply: *write it.* That nudge produced the play he wrote in a weekend and performed at a hospital and a couple of schools. Her name translates to "Mrs. Happiness," which he says is exactly right.

### Jessica — the green high-priestess of the flora
His first real friend in Portugal, older than him; the two of them were the only ones on the 8th-grade honor board at Ibn Mukana. Greenthumbed — she brings him succulents and random plants and her gardening tips, which he absorbs eagerly. She helped him build the Mediterranean garden on his Cacém balcony over years and is still in his life.

### The Mini Cooper he has never driven
In 2010 he won a Mini Cooper Countryman in a contest he entered while spending a summer at his aunt's in Porto. His sister faked a social-security emergency to get the day off and drive him to the dealership. He has never driven it — he had no Portuguese license at the time. It remains undriven. He still wants to drive it to the beach.

### The cats: Elis and Tokischa
He has two cats. Elis, a tabby born around September/October 2016, present since he moved to his current house. Tokischa, a black cat who arrived in March 2022 and is a systematic destroyer of his plants.

### The Herodes solo — being answered by the choir
For three Decembers running (1997–1999) he sang the Herodes solo in the children's Christmas cantata at the Teatro Teresa Carreño. What he remembers is not his own opening lines but the whole choir answering him — calling the character cruel, a murderer, a butcher without equal. Being the one who provoked the entire collective to sing back at him was, in his words, the best part of the solo. He remembers the polyphony, not his own voice.

### Last performance, 2009 — King Ubu, posters and all
His final public performance, at age 22, was an adaptation of Alfred Jarry's *Ubu Roi*. He played King Ubu — a grotesque, camp, absurdist king cuckolded by his queen — and also designed the flyers for the production, the earliest documented instance of his visual/design practice running alongside his voice. After 2009 the voice went underground; he has not performed publicly since.
