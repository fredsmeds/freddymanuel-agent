# CV — Freddy Manuel Roldán Rivero

Visual and multimedia artist. Sintra, Portugal. info@freddymanuel.com · freddymanuel.com.

## Artistic education

- 2008–2013: BA in Visual Arts and Multimedia (180 ECTS), University of Évora,
  Multimedia specialization. Graduated 12 September 2013. Final project: Internércia,
  supervised by Prof. Claudia Giannetti.
- 2004–2007: Pequenos Cantores do Estoril, Mestrina Maria Repas (children's choir in
  Estoril, then Cascais, now the Conservatório de Cascais).
- Admitted by public audition to the Conservatório Nacional de Música de Lisboa, Voice /
  Tenor class; attended one year (audition piece: Selve Amiche, Antonio Caldara,
  rehearsed with Maria Repas).
- 1997–1999: Coro de Niños Cantores de Caracas, Núcleo San Agustín of the Orquesta
  Sinfónica Infantil (later the Orquesta Nacional Infantil de Venezuela), El Sistema,
  Caracas. Three years (two years of choir under Mestrina Irina Capriles and voice
  tutelage of Mestra Tupac Amaru Rivas; one year of classical viola). Annual vocal
  soloist in the núcleo's three Cantata Navideña productions (1997, 1998, 1999), in the
  role of Herod. Concerts in the José Félix Ribas and Ríos Reyna halls of the Teatro
  Teresa Carreño, including the Cantata Criolla (Antonio Estévez) in the Sala Ríos Reyna
  on 12 February 1999, conducted by Felipe Izcaray, with the Orquesta Sinfónica Simón
  Bolívar and the Orquesta Nacional Infantil. Institutional carnet preserved
  (17 September 1999).

## Further training

- 2024: AI Engineering Bootcamp, Ironhack, Lisbon.

## Distinctions and endorsements

- 2024: Hackshow Winner, Ironhack AI Engineering Bootcamp, with the project Diaries of
  the Upheaval, a generative conversational agent with voice cloning.
- 2012: Public in-person endorsement by Joan Fontcuberta of Internércia, at the public
  presentation of the final degree project at the University of Évora (supervised by
  Prof. Claudia Giannetti).

## Screen and stage (selection)

- 2009: Ubu Cornudo (after Alfred Jarry's Ubu Roi), Grupo TEIA (Teatro Eborense
  Independente Amador), Sociedade Recreativa e Dramática Eborense, Évora, in the role of
  King Ubu. Public recording.
- c. 2005–06: Christmas gala, Grande Auditório, Centro Cultural de Belém, Lisbon —
  chorister with the Pequenos Cantores do Estoril in a multi-choir production with live
  orchestra; repertoire from Annie, The Sound of Music and The Wizard of Oz, with
  traditional carols.
- 2004: O Telescópio na Casa da Lua — play written for the Ler-a-par youth reading club
  (Alcabideche municipal library) and performed for patients at Alcoitão Hospital.
- 1990–1997: Television commercials, Caracas — child actor in national television
  advertising: Kellogg's Sonrisa (first commercial), Frito-Lay TazMania Vuelatazos
  (Concept Publicidad, 1995), and multiple campaigns for the clothing brand Graffiti
  (last commercial). First paid work in front of a camera. 35mm reel recovered, 2026.

## Exhibitions and showings (selection)

- 2016: Cosmopolitical City, Latina Middle East Festival, Exhibition of Latin American
  Artists, JW Marriott Marquis, Dubai. Curated by Mariana Turcho.
- 2015: Park, large-format visual intervention (illustrative vinyl panels on glass), Park
  Outdoor Lounge, JW Marriott Marquis, Dubai.
- 2012: Internércia, final degree project, University of Évora. Two-channel video
  installation with a 48-hour performance. Supervised by Prof. Claudia Giannetti.
- 2011: Expensive Title (True Tales), video-performance (DV PAL, 4'23''), II Mostra de
  Vídeo-Performance, 8th edition of the Escrita na Paisagem Festival (theme "Mitologias"),
  Évora, 22–25 September 2011 (Church of S. Vicente; former EPAC granaries). Artistic
  direction: José Alberto Ferreira. Curated by Olga Moreira (Colecção B in partnership
  with CHAIA, University of Évora).
- 2003–2004: Article 21 — Tile mural of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights,
  Inscrire / Inscrever os Direitos do Homem project (Françoise Schein), Escola de
  Alcabideche, four tiles, permanent installation.

## Ongoing series

- 2025–present: Coro Interno — immersive architectural installation with a multi-agent
  artificial intelligence system, video and positional sound. In development. Direct
  continuation of Internércia (2012).
- 2025–present: Cromática Diaspórica y Arrecha — mixed-media series (toner and heat on
  watercolour paper, recomposed on card; and object-pieces on India-ink-painted board).
  First piece in a private collection.
- 2015–2016: Cosmopolitical City — body of 24 works in toner on watercolour paper,
  produced entirely in Dubai.
- 2024: Diaries of the Upheaval — generative conversational agent with voice cloning (RAG
  with LangChain, ChromaDB, Whisper, ElevenLabs). Hackshow Winner, Ironhack.
- 2014: Nepal — photographic series, Kathmandu Valley, Pokhara, Chitwan National Park.

## Collections

Works from the Cosmopolitical City and Cromática Diaspórica y Arrecha series are held in
private collections in Portugal and the United Arab Emirates.
